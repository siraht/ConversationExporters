REFERENCE

How to use it

The short version: stay signed in, refresh provider tabs after extension updates, then sync.

1

Open signed-in tabs

Keep each provider open in this browser. The extension uses the session already in that tab.

2

Capture conversations

Use Sync now beside any provider, or Sync all providers to update every available archive.

3

Keep a portable copy

Download a ZIP, or configure replication to copy changed archive files to storage you control.

How do I recover from a storage quota error?

Click Repair storage to request persistent browser storage and verify a write. This never clears conversations. If blocked, keep the extension installed and replicate or download existing files before changing storage. A new build on disk does not update an already-running temporary extension: reload Conversation Archive at about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox (Firefox/Zen) or chrome://extensions (Chrome), then refresh this dashboard and provider tabs.

What does AI Studio need before its first sync?

After installing or reloading the extension, refresh AI Studio History and open one saved prompt once. This initializes the authenticated inventory and detail requests used by sync.

Where do credentials and conversations go?

The extension uses your signed-in browser session but never reads or exports provider cookies, tokens, or passwords. VPS credentials remain in extension-local storage. Conversations stay in the browser archive unless you download them or enable a replication target.

What does a full sync include?

Sync has no date cutoff. It discovers accessible histories and projects, downloads conversation or prompt bodies and referenced assets, and preserves previous records. Provider APIs may omit content or change behavior; deleted, temporary, expired or inaccessible content cannot be promised. Check the exported validation and sync reports for failures.

Do the counts prove that every chat is saved?

No. Stored counts include retained records, and inventory counts describe what the provider returned. Matching counts alone do not prove account-wide coverage. Confirm known oldest, newest, archived and project conversations after your first full sync. Reports validate the discovered inventory; unsupported provider scopes still require explicit checking.

Does everything stay current automatically?

Hourly background sync covers all five providers while their signed-in tabs are available; the dashboard can be closed. Browser shutdown, revoked permissions or expired sessions can interrupt syncing. Check automatic run status in the activity sidebar. Download or replicate again after syncing to update copies outside the browser.

Do repeated syncs create duplicates?

No. Provider records use stable identities; subsequent syncs retain or replace the existing record when content changes.
